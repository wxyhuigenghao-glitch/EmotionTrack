# Parameters and Measurement Sources
<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:18px">参数与测量来源</div>

## Important distinction: demo proxies vs. validated measures
<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:14px">重要区分：演示用代理指标 vs. 已验证的测量工具</div>

The interface uses short, original slider prompts so the prototype is quick to experience and can be openly shared without presenting itself as a validated questionnaire implementation. The prompts are construct-aligned exploratory variables, not scored copies of the instruments below.

<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">界面采用简短、原创的滑块提示，使原型可以快速体验并公开分享，而不以已验证问卷实现自居。这些提示是与构念对齐的探索性变量，而非下方工具的计分复制版本。</div>

Before a formal study:
<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">在正式研究开始前：</div>

1. select the exact validated instrument and version appropriate to the sample;<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">选择与样本匹配的精确经验证工具及其版本；</span>
2. confirm reuse/licensing and scoring requirements;<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">确认复用、使用许可与计分要求；</span>
3. use validated language translations where required;<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">在需要时使用经过验证的语言翻译；</span>
4. preserve the original wording, response scale, reverse-coded items, and scoring rules;<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">保留原始措辞、反应量表、反向计分条目与计分规则；</span>
5. pilot measurement invariance and comprehension across language/cultural groups;<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">跨语言、文化群体预试测量不变性与理解程度；</span>
6. preregister which variables are confirmatory and which are exploratory.<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">预注册哪些变量属于验证性、哪些属于探索性。</span>

## Optional browser-derived signals
<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:14px">可选浏览器衍生信号</div>

| Event<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">事件</span> | Sampling<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">采样</span> | Meaning in this demo<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">在本演示中的含义</span> | Limitation<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">局限</span> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sensor.audio_rms` | ~3.3 Hz | root-mean-square audio amplitude<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">均方根音频振幅</span> | Not speech emotion recognition; affected by distance and environmental noise<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">并非语音情绪识别，受距离与环境噪声影响</span> |
| `sensor.camera_proxy.luminance` | ~1.7 Hz | mean sampled frame brightness<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">采样帧的平均亮度</span> | Used for quality/context monitoring, not affect inference<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">用于质量、情境监控，而非情绪推断</span> |
| `sensor.camera_proxy.motion` | ~1.7 Hz | coarse inter-frame pixel difference<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">粗略的帧间像素差</span> | Not face, gaze, posture, or action recognition<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">并非人脸、视线、姿态或动作识别</span> |
| `voice_response.completed` | event based | press-and-hold response duration<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">按住回应的持续时间</span> | No raw audio or transcript<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">不保存原始音频或转写</span> |
| decision/clue events | event based | choice, inspection order, and timing<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">选择、查看顺序与时机</span> | Behaviour must be interpreted within the scripted scene<br><span style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:12px">行为须在脚本化场景内解释</span> |

For formal acoustic modelling, a standardised feature set such as eGeMAPS may provide a more defensible starting point than the amplitude-only demo proxy [7]. Facial action, gaze, body movement, HR/HRV, EDA, and device/activity context require dedicated validated pipelines and synchronisation; they are intentionally not simulated as if measured here.

<div style="color:#9ca3af;font-family:'SimSun','宋体',serif;font-size:13px">对于正式的声学建模，诸如 eGeMAPS 这样的标准化特征集相比仅依赖振幅的演示代理，可能是更稳健的起点 [7]。面部动作、视线、身体运动、HR/HRV、EDA 以及设备、活动情境需要专门的经验证流程与同步校准；本演示有意不将它们模拟为已测量。</div>
